# DOONY — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 29 August 2026**

DOONY is a personal record-keeping app for tracking which calendar days were
spent inside or outside New York State, and for organizing supporting domicile
documentation.

## The short version

**DOONY collects nothing.** It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no
advertising. It makes no network requests of any kind. Everything you record
stays on your iPhone until you deliberately export it yourself.

## What the app handles

To do its job, DOONY records and stores on your device:

- **Location.** Your device's location, including in the background, is used to
  determine whether each calendar day was spent inside or outside New York
  State. Location readings and the resulting day classifications are stored on
  the device.
- **Information you enter.** Details you type into the domicile section —
  addresses, vehicles, licenses, memberships, advisors, property, and similar
  records.
- **Documents you attach.** Photographs and files you choose to attach as
  supporting evidence.

## What happens to it

Nothing leaves your iPhone. DOONY contains no networking code: it cannot send
your location, your records, or your documents anywhere, because it has no
ability to make a network connection at all.

The only way any data leaves the device is when **you** initiate an export from
the Export tab and choose a destination through the standard iOS share sheet —
for example, mailing a report to your own accountant. What happens to the file
after that is governed by whatever service you chose to send it through, not by
DOONY.

## How it is protected on the device

- The app's data store is protected with iOS Data Protection
  (`NSFileProtectionComplete`), so it is encrypted while the device is locked.
- Attached documents are individually encrypted with AES-GCM, using a key held
  in the iOS Keychain.
- Location metadata (EXIF) is stripped from photographs you attach.

## Third parties

There are none. DOONY includes no third-party SDKs, no analytics or crash
reporting services, no advertising networks, and no tracking of any kind. No
data is shared with anyone, because no data is collected.

## Children

DOONY is not directed at children and does not knowingly handle information
about them.

## Your control over the data

All data lives on your device. You can edit or delete individual records inside
the app, and deleting the app removes all of its data from the device
permanently. Because nothing is transmitted anywhere, there is no copy held by
the developer to request, correct, or delete.

You can revoke DOONY's access to your location at any time in **Settings →
Privacy & Security → Location Services**. The app will stop recording new days;
existing records remain on the device.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will change with it.
