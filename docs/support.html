Getting started

  • On first launch, DOONY asks for location access. Choose Allow While Using App, then Change to Always Allow when iOS follows up.
  • Always access is what makes the day count complete. With "While Using", days you don't open the app are recorded as Unverified.
  • Enable Precise Location. Approximate location is not accurate enough near a state border.

Common questions

A day shows as "Unverified." No location fix was recorded that day — usually the phone was off, in Airplane Mode, or location access was switched off. DOONY never guesses. Open the day and use Manual override to set it yourself, backing it up with your own records.

A day near the state line looks wrong. Days recorded within about 2 km of the border are flagged as near-border and captured with high-accuracy GPS. If one is still wrong, open the day, check the raw samples, and use Manual override.

Does my data go anywhere? No. DOONY makes no network requests at all. Everything stays on your iPhone, encrypted at rest. See the privacy policy. The only way data leaves the device is an export you start yourself from the Export tab.

How do I get my records out? The Export tab produces CSV files and a PDF report, handed to the iOS share sheet so you can mail them to your accountant or save them to Files.

Will it drain my battery? It shouldn't. DOONY relies on low-power significant-location-change monitoring and a single geofence, and only turns on high-accuracy GPS within about 2 km of the New York border.

What happens if I delete the app? All of its data is deleted with it, permanently. There is no cloud backup, so export anything you need first.

Important

DOONY is a record-keeping aid. It is not legal or tax advice, and it is not proof of residency or domicile. A GPS log is one corroborating source among several an auditor will weigh. Decisions about your residency belong with your accountant or attorney.
